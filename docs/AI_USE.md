# Declaration of AI-assisted tools

*Spectrochimica Acta Part A* requires disclosure of generative AI use in the
writing process and, separately, of AI-generated or AI-assisted artwork. This
file records what was used in preparing this repository and the associated
manuscript, so the statement in the submission can be checked against it.

## Artwork

- **Figure 1** (experimental workflow diagram) and the **graphical abstract**
  were drafted with AI assistance from author-supplied specifications and
  author-verified numerical results. Both are schematic explanatory artwork:
  they contain no measured data that is not also reported in a table, and no
  image was synthesised from a photographic generative model.
- All other figures (`results/paper_figures/`) are produced directly by the
  scripts in `src/` from the experimental outputs, without AI involvement.

## Text

Any AI assistance used in drafting or editing manuscript text is disclosed in
the manuscript's own declaration section, in the form the journal requires.

## Analysis

No generative AI system selected wavelengths, trained a model, computed a
metric, or produced any number reported in the manuscript. Every value comes
from the scripts in `src/` executed on the GreenHyperSpectra data, and the
resulting CSVs are committed under `results/paper_tables/` for inspection.

## Responsibility

The authors reviewed and verified all AI-assisted output and take full
responsibility for the content of the manuscript, the figures and this
repository.
